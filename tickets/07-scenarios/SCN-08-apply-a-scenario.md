## Story

As a **household member**, I want **to write a scenario's changes into my real plan** so that **a decision I have made stops being hypothetical**.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Applying is explicit and confirmed, listing exactly what will change
- [ ] Every override becomes a real edit to configuration
- [ ] Nothing is applied until confirmed
- [ ] After applying, the scenario no longer shows a delta against the baseline
- [ ] The dashboard reflects the change immediately
