## Story

As a **employee**, I want **to record my monthly net and income tax** so that **my salary counts toward household income**.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Monthly net before tax, monthly income tax, start date, optional end date
- [ ] Net after tax is the difference between them
- [ ] Optional annual gross is informational and never enters any calculation
- [ ] No gross-to-net payroll engine is implemented
