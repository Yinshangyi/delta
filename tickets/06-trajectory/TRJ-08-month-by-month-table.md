## Story

As a **household member**, I want **to read the projection month by month** so that **I can see exactly where the money goes**.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Starting savings, income, commitments, net and ending savings per month
- [ ] Past months marked actual, future forecast, by more than colour
- [ ] A row expands to show its constituent cash flows, itemised and labelled
- [ ] A way to reach the goal month without paging through every row
- [ ] Reconstructed figures for past months are not presented as recorded fact

## Notes

§31 already carries `cashFlows` per month. Without the expansion, a month showing €14,753 against a usual €8,340 is unexplainable from anywhere in the app.
