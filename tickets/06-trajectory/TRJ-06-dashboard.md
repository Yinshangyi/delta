## Story

As a **household member**, I want **one screen answering when we reach the goal** so that **I get the answer without navigating anywhere**.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] The estimated target date dominates the composition
- [ ] Total capital against the goal, with the percentage reached
- [ ] Net worth beneath it where debts exist; absent where none do
- [ ] Current trajectory: household income, commitments, expected monthly savings
- [ ] The expected-savings figure states honestly whether scheduled tax is included
- [ ] The projection chart
- [ ] Commitments with a trajectory of their own — debts and upcoming tax — under a heading, with an empty case
- [ ] Exactly one primary action: update balances
- [ ] The target date is labelled an estimate

## Notes

`size: L` — split by tier. The expected-savings definition matters: excluding scheduled tax without saying so overstates the near-term rate several-fold.
