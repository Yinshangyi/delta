## Story

As a **household member**, I want **to know whether we are ahead of or behind the forecast** so that **I can react before the target date drifts far**.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] The latest actual is compared with what was forecast for that date
- [ ] The variance is stated in money and in target-date movement
- [ ] Ahead and behind are distinguished by more than colour
- [ ] The tone stays level — behind plan is information, not failure
- [ ] With no prior forecast to compare against, the state says so rather than showing zero
