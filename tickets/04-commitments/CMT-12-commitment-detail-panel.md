## Story

As a **household member**, I want **to see and edit one commitment in depth** so that **each type shows what actually matters about it**.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Debt shows initial against remaining, progress, percent repaid, payment, months remaining and payoff date
- [ ] Tax shows its full dated schedule
- [ ] The panel holds the commitment totals when nothing is selected, so the column never collapses
- [ ] List rows show a hover state indicating they are selectable
- [ ] Destructive actions sit apart from the primary action
